About Me

Hi! I'm Akram Albayhaqi, an undergraduate student majoring in Multimedia Education at Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia. I specialize in interactive media such as video games, applications, and websites. Over the years, I've developed a wide range of skills including design, photography, videography, editing, and both 2D & 3D animation.

I'm especially passionate about game development—not just playing games, but analyzing the small details that make gameplay memorable and meaningful. I love exploring unique mechanics, aesthetics, and storytelling techniques that bring digital experiences to life.

My dream is to create a game or digital product that gains national recognition and brings real value to its users. Whether it's entertainment, education, or something entirely new, I'm always excited to build something impactful. While some of my skills are stronger than others, I'm constantly learning and striving to improve in every area.

Thanks for visiting my site—feel free to explore my work and reach out if you'd like to connect or collaborate!
